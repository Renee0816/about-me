About me

As a Certified Reverse Mortgage Professional (CRMP), I've spent my career guiding clients, many of them older homeowners, through one of the most important and heavily regulated financial decisions of their lives. That work takes patience, precision, and the ability to explain complicated topics in plain language.

Now I'm moving into AI support engineering: helping customers get real value from AI products, troubleshooting technical issues, and turning user feedback into better documentation and better products. I take the same thing with me: people deserve clear answers and someone who follows through.
